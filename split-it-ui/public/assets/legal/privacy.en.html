2. Data we collect and why

  • Account: name and email — to create your account and authenticate you.
  • Authentication: password hash (PBKDF2) or Google identifier — secure sign-in.
  • Authorization: date, policy version and IP — to prove your consent.
  • Activity: groups, expenses, amounts, participants and settlements — to run shared expenses.
  • Security: audit log (who did what and when) — traceability and security.

We do not collect sensitive data, data from minors, or financial information. SplitIt does not process payments; it only records agreements between users.

3. Your authorization

Before collecting your data we ask for your prior, express and informed authorization. At registration you give it through a non-pre-checked box; when you sign in with Google for the first time you give it by continuing, after reading the authorization notice shown above the Google button. You may revoke it at any time, unless we are legally required to keep the data.

4. Your rights

You can know, update, rectify and delete your data, request proof of your authorization, be informed about its use, revoke the authorization, and file complaints before the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).

In the app you can download your data and delete your account from the header menu, or write to [correo@dominio].

5. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. After deletion, your account is anonymized and your financial records are kept in anonymized form to preserve the other members' balances and for legal/accounting purposes.

6. Service providers

We use infrastructure providers (hosting, database, email) that may be located outside Colombia and act as data processors under confidentiality agreements.

7. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), PBKDF2 password hashing, least-privilege database access, an audit trail and verified backups.

8. Contact

Write to [correo@dominio]. Consultations are answered within 10 business days and claims within 15 business days.
